# Tax audit checklist — section 44AB

Client: ______________________  FY: __________  AY: __________

## Applicability

- [ ] Turnover or gross receipts computed, and the applicable threshold confirmed
- [ ] Cash receipts and cash payments tested against the 5% limit where the
      higher turnover threshold is being relied on
- [ ] Presumptive taxation position under 44AD/44ADA/44AE considered
- [ ] Correct form identified — 3CA where accounts are audited under another
      law, otherwise 3CB

## Records obtained

- [ ] Signed financial statements for the year
- [ ] Trial balance agreeing to the financial statements
- [ ] Ledgers, and bank statements for every account
- [ ] Previous year audit report and 3CD
- [ ] Fixed asset register with additions and deletions for the year
- [ ] Stock records and closing stock valuation working
- [ ] GST returns for the year, and the reconciliation to the books
- [ ] TDS returns for all four quarters, and challans

## Key 3CD clauses

- [ ] Clause 13 — method of accounting, and any change from the previous year
- [ ] Clause 14 — stock valuation method, applied consistently
- [ ] Clause 16 — amounts not credited to the profit and loss account
- [ ] Clause 17 — property transferred below the stamp duty value (43CA/50C)
- [ ] Clause 18 — depreciation as per the Income Tax Act, block-wise
- [ ] Clause 20 — bonus, commission, and employee contributions
- [ ] Clause 21 — inadmissible expenditure, personal expenses, and penalties
- [ ] Clause 21(b) — payments disallowed under 40(a) for TDS default
- [ ] Clause 21(d) — cash payments over the section 40A(3) limit
- [ ] Clause 26 — section 43B items, and whether paid before the due date
- [ ] Clause 31 — loans and deposits taken or repaid (269SS/269T/269ST)
- [ ] Clause 34 — TDS compliance, deduction and deposit, statement-wise
- [ ] Clause 44 — expenditure break-up between registered and unregistered
      suppliers under GST

## Completion

- [ ] Management representation letter obtained and signed
- [ ] Working papers indexed and cross-referenced to the 3CD
- [ ] Queries cleared, or reported where unresolved
- [ ] Draft report reviewed by the principal before upload
- [ ] UDIN generated after the report is signed
- [ ] Report uploaded and accepted by the assessee on the portal
