# Monthly GST return checklist

Client: ______________________  Period: ____________  GSTIN: ____________

## Before filing

- [ ] Sales register tallied with books for the period
- [ ] B2B invoices checked for correct GSTIN of the recipient
- [ ] Invoice series continuous, with no missing or duplicate numbers
- [ ] Credit and debit notes for the period included
- [ ] Export and SEZ supplies flagged correctly, with LUT reference if applicable
- [ ] Place of supply verified — CGST/SGST versus IGST correctly applied
- [ ] Rate-wise summary agrees with the sales register totals

## Input tax credit

- [ ] GSTR-2B downloaded for the period
- [ ] Purchase register reconciled against 2B
- [ ] Invoices in books but not in 2B listed and followed up with suppliers
- [ ] Ineligible credit under section 17(5) identified and excluded
- [ ] Reversal under Rule 42/43 computed where there are exempt supplies
- [ ] Credit on invoices older than the section 16(4) time limit excluded

## Payment and filing

- [ ] Output tax less eligible ITC computed, and cash requirement confirmed
- [ ] Interest on any late payment computed
- [ ] Challan paid and reflected in the cash ledger before filing
- [ ] GSTR-1 filed and acknowledgement saved
- [ ] GSTR-3B filed and acknowledgement saved
- [ ] Filed figures agreed back to the books
