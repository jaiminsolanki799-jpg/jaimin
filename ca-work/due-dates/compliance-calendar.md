# Compliance calendar

Standard statutory due dates under Indian direct tax, GST, and company law.

> **Verify before relying on any date here.** These are the dates as they stand
> under the statute. CBDT, CBIC, and MCA extend them by notification most years,
> sometimes only days before the deadline, and sometimes only for certain
> categories or states. Always confirm against the current notification on the
> relevant portal before acting.

## Every month

| Day | Compliance |
| --- | --- |
| 7th | TDS/TCS payment for the previous month (for March, the due date is 30 April) |
| 10th | GSTR-7 (TDS under GST) and GSTR-8 (TCS by e-commerce operators) |
| 11th | GSTR-1 — monthly filers |
| 13th | IFF — QRMP filers opting to upload B2B invoices monthly |
| 15th | PF and ESI payment for the previous month |
| 20th | GSTR-3B — monthly filers |
| 25th | PMT-06 — QRMP monthly tax payment |

QRMP filers file GSTR-1 quarterly by the 13th of the month following the
quarter, and GSTR-3B quarterly by the 22nd or 24th depending on the state
group they fall in.

## Every quarter

| Quarter | TDS return (24Q / 26Q / 27Q) | Advance tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apr–Jun | 31 July | 15 June — 15% cumulative |
| Jul–Sep | 31 October | 15 September — 45% cumulative |
| Oct–Dec | 31 January | 15 December — 75% cumulative |
| Jan–Mar | 31 May | 15 March — 100% cumulative |

Form 16A (TDS certificate for non-salary payments) is due within 15 days of the
TDS return due date.

## Every year

| Date | Compliance |
| --- | --- |
| 15 June | Form 16 issued to employees |
| 31 July | ITR — assessees not subject to audit |
| 30 September | Tax audit report under section 44AB (Form 3CA/3CB with 3CD) |
| 30 September | DIR-3 KYC for directors |
| 31 October | ITR — assessees subject to audit |
| 30 November | ITR — assessees with transfer pricing obligations (Form 3CEB) |
| 31 December | GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for the previous financial year |
| 31 December | Belated and revised returns for the previous assessment year |

### Company and LLP filings

| Form | Due |
| --- | --- |
| ADT-1 | Within 15 days of the AGM |
| AOC-4 | Within 30 days of the AGM |
| MGT-7 / MGT-7A | Within 60 days of the AGM |
| LLP Form 11 | 30 May |
| LLP Form 8 | 30 October |

An AGM must be held within six months of the financial year end, so for a
31 March year end the outer date is 30 September, and the filing dates above run
from the date the AGM is actually held.

## Notes

- A due date falling on a Sunday or a notified holiday does not usually shift on
  the portals; plan to file the working day before.
- Interest under sections 234A/234B/234C and late fees under section 234F run
  independently of any extension of the filing date unless the notification says
  otherwise — an extended filing date does not always extend the payment date.
- Track dates per client in `../clients/`, not here. This file is the generic
  reference.
